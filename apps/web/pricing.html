Pricing & billing

Free up to 50GB pledged to buddies, forever. Past that, it's a flat $3 a month for any pledge size, whether that's 100GB or 20TB. Every plan includes 10GB a month of relay bandwidth; on the paid plan, relay past that is $0.02 per GB. Direct transfers between your device and your buddy's are always free.

What you're paying for

You and your buddy bring the disks, so you're not renting storage from us. The $3 covers the service around it: accounts, pairing and invites, monitoring and alerts, the dashboard, and a relay to fall back on when your devices can't reach each other directly. That's why the price doesn't grow with how much you pledge.

Free

  • Up to 50GB pledged across all your buddy pairings
  • 10GB of relay bandwidth a month. Past that, buddy syncing pauses until the 1st of the next month, or until you subscribe
  • No card needed

Paid: $3 / month

  • Pledge as much as you and your buddies have room for
  • 10GB of relay bandwidth a month included, then $0.02 / GB, metered on actual bytes relayed, with no monthly limit
  • One invoice and one card charge a month

Most transfers go directly, peer-to-peer, between your device and your buddy's, and those never count toward anything. The relay only steps in when a direct connection can't be made (strict NAT, corporate firewall, some carrier networks).

How pledged storage is counted

A "pledge" is the amount you've agreed to store on your buddy's behalf, set when an invite is created and redeemed. Your total is the sum of every active pairing you're part of, whichever side of it you're on:

  1. You pair with a buddy for, say, 500GB. That pairing now counts 500GB toward both of your pledged totals. It's the same number on both sides, since you're each agreeing to store that much for the other.
  2. Up to 50GB in total is free. If all your pairings add up to 50GB or less, you never need a subscription at all.
  3. Past 50GB, you need the paid plan. Pledging more than the free allowance (by creating an invite or redeeming one) asks you to subscribe first. After that, the price stays $3 a month however many buddies you add and however big the pledges get.

How relay usage is measured and billed

  1. Your client tracks every byte's path. For each transfer, it knows whether the data went directly to your buddy's device or had to be relayed.
  2. It reports relay usage roughly every 30 seconds. Just the megabytes relayed since the last report, never file contents, since the relay itself never sees anything but already-encrypted ciphertext.
  3. The first 10GB each calendar month is included. On the free plan that's the cap. On the paid plan, only usage past it counts toward a charge, and usage from before you subscribed that month counts toward the 10GB too.
  4. We sync billable usage to Stripe every 15 minutes. This just records a "meter event", a data point, not a charge. Nothing happens to your card at this point.
  5. Once a month, at your normal renewal date, it's all totaled up. Stripe adds up that billing period's meter events into a single relay-bandwidth line item, next to the $3 plan, on one invoice.
  6. Your card is charged once. Never per report, never per relay session: one combined charge per month, same as any ordinary subscription renewal.

Worked example

Say you pledge 2TB to a buddy (so they're pledging 2TB back to you too: one pairing, same number both ways), and over the course of a month your network's NAT blocks direct connections often enough that 15GB of your transfers went through the relay instead.

Item Amount Rate Cost
Paid plan 2 TB pledged flat $3.00
Relay bandwidth, included 10 GB $0 / GB $0.00
Relay bandwidth, past the allowance 5 GB $0.02 / GB $0.10
Total charged this month $3.10

That $3.10 is a single charge on your card at your subscription's normal monthly renewal date. If that same month your direct connection had worked for every transfer, or the relay had carried 10GB or less, you'd pay $3.00.

What never costs anything

  • Pledges of 50GB or less, total. No subscription, no card on file, nothing.
  • Direct peer-to-peer traffic. The normal case for most home and office networks. Only bytes that go through the relay are ever counted.
  • The first 10GB of relay each month, on either plan.
  • Backing up, restoring, and checking buddy status. These aren't separately billed actions; the plan (and relay, if used) already covers them.
