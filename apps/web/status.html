Feature status

An honest, up-to-date list of what Backup Buddies actually does today, and what's still being built or tested. If you're wondering whether something works, or what its limits are, it should be answered here rather than requiring a support ticket.

Available now

Verified against real, live devices — not just "it compiles."

  • Available Automatic encrypted backup Every ~30 seconds, new or changed files in your backup folder are encrypted on your device and sent to your buddy. Deleting a file locally removes it from your buddy's live copy too (older versions stay recoverable).
  • Available Direct peer-to-peer transfer, with automatic relay fallback Your device and your buddy's device try to connect directly first. On the same local network, that's instant and automatic. Over the internet, it depends on both sides' network/router — when a direct connection isn't possible, your data still transfers reliably through our relay, which can never read it (see "Known limitations" below for when this applies).
  • Available Restore, including file history Restore a single file, restore everything your buddy has for you at once, or pull back a specific earlier version of a file you've since deleted — with a live progress indicator while it runs.
  • Available Pledge-based storage limits Each buddy pairing has an agreed storage size. Going over it is refused automatically, and your dashboard tells you exactly why a file didn't send (pledge full, file too large, buddy's disk full).
  • Available Drift detection (reconciliation) Roughly every 10 minutes, your device double-checks that what your buddy actually has on disk still matches what it expects — catching anything lost or changed outside the normal backup flow — and automatically re-sends anything missing.
  • Available Unreachable-buddy detection If your buddy's device genuinely can't be reached, your dashboard flags it as stale rather than silently showing "backup ok" — backed by a real connectivity check, not an assumption.
  • Available Dashboard Per-buddy backup status, your device's build/uptime/local file count, each buddy's real disk usage, and a lifetime direct-vs-relay bandwidth breakdown.
  • Available Find a buddy Browse public listings at an exact storage size, with optional self-reported country, upload-speed tier, always-on, and relay-only info to help you judge fit before pairing.
  • Available Free tier The first 50GB you pledge to buddies is free, no card required.

Known limitations & in progress

Nothing here is secret or hidden — just not finished, not yet proven under real conditions, or an inherent limit we want to be upfront about.

  • Limitation Direct connections aren't guaranteed on every network Whether a direct connection is possible depends on your router/network's NAT behavior. Many home networks and local networks work great. Some networks — strict corporate firewalls especially — block the hole-punching direct connections need entirely, forwarded port or not. When that happens, your backups still complete normally through our relay; this is expected, graceful behavior, not a failure.
  • In progress Marketplace listing filters Country, upload speed, "usually online", and "relay-only" are currently shown on listings to help you judge a buddy yourself, but don't yet filter or auto-match search results.
  • In progress Low-disk protection Your device is designed to refuse new incoming files once its own disk runs low, independent of pledge size — built, but not yet exercised against a real low-disk machine.
  • In progress Relay bandwidth billing Relay usage is metered and reported on paid accounts; full end-to-end confirmation against billing is still being verified.
  • Limitation Fixed backup interval, no file filtering Backups run on a fixed ~30 second cycle and back up everything in your configured folder — there's no way yet to change the interval or exclude specific files/patterns.
  • Limitation One buddy per pledge Each pledge is a 1:1 relationship with a single buddy — there's no way to share one backup folder across multiple buddies at once.
