Not quite cloud storage. Not quite Syncthing.

Backup Buddies borrows the direct-connection tech behind tools like ZeroTier and the peer-to-peer philosophy of Syncthing, but points them at a different problem: real backup redundancy with another person, not just syncing your own devices.

Cloud storage (S3, Backblaze, etc.) Syncthing / ZeroTier Backup Buddies
What it is You rent storage from a company You sync or network your own devices together You trade storage with someone else's device
Pricing Pay per GB stored, every month, forever — plus egress fees Free, but it's bring-your-own storage and hardware Free up to 50GB pledged, including 10GB/month of relay. Past that, $3/TB/month on what you pledge, plus $0.02/GB for relay you can't avoid. No egress fees, and direct transfers are always free
Connection Always through their servers Direct peer-to-peer between your devices, with a relay/mesh fallback Same idea — direct first, relay fallback — but between two different people's devices
Who's on the other end A company's data center Just you, across your own devices A buddy you chose, backing each other up
Encryption Varies by provider and plan Varies by setup Always, before it leaves your device — nobody, including us, can read it

Think of it as ZeroTier's direct-connection tech and Syncthing's peer-to-peer philosophy, aimed at a different problem: instead of syncing your own devices, you pair with someone else's for real backup redundancy — at cloud-storage reliability, without the monthly bill.

What the client looks like

Every device also runs its own local dashboard — disk, bandwidth, and each buddy's pledge at a glance, plus a file browser to restore anything (or roll back to an earlier version).
