Getting started

Everything you need to go from nothing to two machines actually backing each other up, in order. Each step links to where you'll do it.

1. Create your account

Sign up with an email and password, or with Google — either way you'll need to accept the Terms of Service, which spells out that data stored with a buddy is at your own risk (see Terms for the full picture of what that means and what doesn't change).

2. Find a buddy

You need someone to trade storage with before anything else here is useful. From your dashboard, you have two options:

  • Invite someone you know — pick how much storage to pledge, generate a code under Invite a buddy, and send it to them directly.
  • Find someone publicly — check "List this publicly" when creating your invite to list it in Find a buddy, or browse that same list to request a pairing with someone who's already listed space.

Either way, a pairing becomes active the moment the other side redeems it — no approval step beyond that.

3. Check your billing

The first 50GB you pledge (combined across all your pairings) is free. Past that, it's $3/TB/month, billed automatically based on how much you've pledged — not how much you've actually used. Check your estimated cost and subscribe (if needed) under Billing. If you're staying under 50GB, you can skip this — nothing to subscribe to yet.

4. Set up the client on your machine(s)

This is the part that actually moves data. Once you have an active pairing:

  • Add a device under Devices on your dashboard — this gives you a token.
  • Follow the full walkthrough on the Client setup page to install and run it with that token.
  • Do this on every machine you want backed up. Your buddy needs to do the same on theirs — both sides have to be running the client for anything to happen.

5. Confirm it's working

Once both clients are running, your dashboard's Devices card will show each device as connected, and the client's own logs will show a successful connectivity check roughly every 30 seconds. See the Client setup page's troubleshooting section if that's not happening.

What's not built yet

Right now the client proves the connection works end to end with real encryption — it doesn't yet watch a folder and back it up automatically, or let you restore files. That's the next thing being built. This page will be updated as that lands.
