Set up the client

The website handles your account, billing, and finding a buddy. The actual backup happens through a small program you run on your own computer, in Docker. Here's how to get it running.

Before you start

You'll need:

  • Docker installed and running on the machine you want to back up.
  • An active buddy pairing — redeem an invite code or request one from Find a buddy first. The client won't have anyone to talk to without one.
  • A passphrase only you'll know. Think of it now — it encrypts your data before it leaves your machine, and there's no way to recover it if you lose it. A few random words work well.

Steps

  1. Get a device token On your dashboard, under Devices, give this machine a label (e.g. "home desktop") and click Add device. You'll see a token — copy it now, it's only shown once.
  2. Download the client files Clone or download apps/client/ from the project's repository — it contains docker-compose.yml and .env.example. (If you don't have access to the repository, ask whoever set up your account for these two files.)
  3. Fill in your .env file Copy .env.example to .env and fill in DEVICE_TOKEN (from step 1) and BACKUP_PASSPHRASE (your own — never sent to us or your buddy). Leave API_URL and RELAY_URL as they are.
  4. Start it In that folder, run: 
    docker compose up -d
docker compose logs -f
    You should see a line confirming the device registered. Your Devices card on the dashboard will now show this device as connected.
  5. Confirm it found your buddy Once your buddy's client is running too, you'll see a "connectivity test succeeded" line in the logs roughly every 30 seconds. That confirms your two machines can reach each other — directly, or through the relay if a direct connection isn't possible.

What this version actually does

This first release of the client proves the pipe works end to end — your machine, your buddy's machine, and the relay in between — using the same client-side encryption real backups will use. It is not yet doing full folder backup and restore; that's the next piece being built on top of this foundation. If you're trying this out, expect a "connectivity test succeeded" log line, not a working backup yet.

Troubleshooting

  • No "registered" line in the logs: double-check DEVICE_TOKEN was copied correctly and the container can reach api.filegarden.net.
  • Registered, but no connectivity test: confirm your buddy pairing shows as "active" on your dashboard, and that your buddy has also completed these steps on their end.
  • Lost your device token: remove the device on your dashboard and add it again — tokens can't be retrieved after creation, only replaced.
